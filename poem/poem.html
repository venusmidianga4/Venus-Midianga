An Angel’s Rhythm

I wish I was an angel so I could fly
Release me from the external expectations
For which reality am I meant to conform to?
What is my purpose? Will love destroy me or create me?

Discipline & Desire

I will harvest to the bone and be disciplined in order to feel free,
to find peace.
An angel who is motivated and energized by love.

Contradictions I Carry

  • My mistakes will be louder than my presence
  • And still will be capable of healing
  • For love has no definition

What Is Owned?

For the things I achieve and own are not mine.
My body and mind will move forward,
no matter how capable I am.

Movement
  1. I will fly away to my own rhythm
  2. My love for others may be heaven-sent
  3. I am an angel with wings
Embodiment

In a Black woman’s body.
